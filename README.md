# Build a Luxury Website for Charclyr Cleaning

Create an ultra-premium, elegant, modern, fully responsive website for **Charclyr Cleaning – Better Living.**

### Business Information
- Business Name: Charclyr Cleaning
- Phone: 561-574-0921
- Service Area: Palm Beach County, Florida
- Main Service: Residential Cleaning
- Tagline: “A Cleaner Home Means a Calmer You.”
- Brand Message: Reliable & Trustworthy, Attention to Detail, Your Satisfaction Matters.

### Design Direction
Use a sophisticated luxury palette:
- Rich Black: #080808
- Champagne Gold: #D6B36A
- Warm Ivory: #F8F4EA
- Soft Beige: #E8DDC8

Create a premium visual identity with elegant typography, refined gold accents, cinematic photography, subtle animations, beautiful spacing, glass effects used sparingly, and sophisticated hover interactions.

### Hero Banner – Make It Extraordinary
Design a unique, visually striking luxury hero—not a generic cleaning website banner. Use a cinematic, high-end modern living room background with a refined black-and-gold composition. Feature the business name prominently, a powerful headline, elegant tagline, and premium CTA buttons: “Get a Free Quote” and “Call Now.” Add subtle gold motion effects and a polished layout. Make the first screen feel like an expensive professional brand.

### Website Sections
1. **About Charclyr** – A welcoming introduction to the cleaning business.
2. **Our Cleaning Services** – One-Time, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out, Window, Post-Construction, and Janitorial Cleaning.
3. **Why Choose Us** – Reliability, attention to detail, flexible cleaning schedules, and customer satisfaction.
4. **Before & After** – A premium interactive comparison section with exactly two comparison pairs, using realistic cleaning photos.
5. **Cleaning Reels** – A stylish vertical video gallery with 3 empty video placeholders for manually adding videos later. Do not add sample videos.
6. **Premium Gallery** – Integrate the supplied Image Fan Carousel component as a unique interactive visual gallery. Use relevant luxury home and cleaning images.
7. **Testimonials** – Integrate the supplied Testimonials 3 component. Do not use its fake celebrity reviews. Create editable testimonial placeholders and only display genuine customer reviews when provided.
8. **Service Area Map** – Add an interactive map clearly showing Palm Beach County, Florida. Do not invent a street address.
9. **Contact & Free Quote** – Premium contact section with phone number 561-574-0921, service area, and an easy quote-request form.
10. **Footer** – Elegant black-and-gold footer with business name, navigation, phone, and service area.

### Component Integration – Important
Integrate both supplied components:
- `image-fan-carousel.tsx` – Use in the Premium Gallery section.
- `testimonials-3.tsx` – Use in the Testimonials section.

Place components in `/components/ui`. Ensure compatibility with the existing React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn structure. Install only necessary dependencies and adapt Next.js-specific Image usage if the project uses Vite.

Also install the supplied Avatar dependency where needed and preserve the provided component designs and animations.

### Final Requirements
- Make every section visually unique; avoid repetitive card layouts.
- Use premium, relevant, high-quality cleaning and interior photography.
- Ensure excellent desktop, tablet, and mobile responsiveness.
- Add smooth scroll reveals and refined micro-interactions.
- Keep all business information editable.
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, awards, addresses, or business claims.
- Keep the website fast, accessible, and SEO-friendly.
- Do not create unnecessary sections or change the brand identity.

**Final goal:** A truly high-end, memorable cleaning company website that looks custom-designed by a premium professional web design agency—not a generic template.
